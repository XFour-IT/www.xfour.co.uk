A managed IT retainer is often sold as cover for when something breaks: a laptop won't start, a printer drops off the network, someone is locked out of email. That is the visible half of the job, and it is the half most quotes describe.

The other half is the work nobody notices until it is missing — patching that actually runs on schedule, backups that get test-restored rather than just taken, and an up-to-date record of who has access to what. A retainer that only covers the first half looks identical to one that covers both, right up until it doesn't.
