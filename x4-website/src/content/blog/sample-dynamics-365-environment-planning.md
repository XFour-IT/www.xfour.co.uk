Most Dynamics 365 projects start with a conversation about licences, because licences are the number with a price on them. The environment plan — how many sandboxes, who can change what, and how changes move from a sandbox into production — tends to come later, if it comes up at all.

That order is where the rework we get called in to fix usually starts. A licence count decided before the environment plan exists almost always has to be revisited once the plan is written, because the plan is what tells you how many environments, and therefore how many of certain licence types, the project actually needs.
