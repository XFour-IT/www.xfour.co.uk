About XFour

Microsoft Dynamics 365 consultancy and managed IT support for small businesses in Reading and across the Thames Valley.

What we do

XFour works with small businesses in two ways.

Microsoft Dynamics 365. We help you choose, set up and run Dynamics 365 so that your customer records, your sales process and your support conversations live in one place instead of several spreadsheets.

Managed IT. We look after the day-to-day: user accounts, devices, email, backups and security, so that your team can get on with its work and you have someone to call when something breaks.

We also take on project management and web design work for the clients we support.
