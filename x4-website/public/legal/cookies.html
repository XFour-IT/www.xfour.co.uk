Cookie Policy

What this website stores on your device, who puts it there, and how to change your mind.

The short version

This site works without cookies. Nothing that needs your permission is stored until you give it.

The one thing that needs your permission is our live chat. It is run for us by Microsoft, and it stores things in your browser as soon as it loads. So it stays switched off until you choose to allow it. If you do not allow it, the chat bubble does not appear and you can still reach us by phone or email.

We also count visits, but in a way that stores nothing on your device and does not identify you. That is explained under Measurement that stores nothing.

Measurement that stores nothing

Two tools count visits to this site. Neither sets a cookie and neither writes anything to your device, so neither one needs your permission under the cookie rules. We list them anyway, because you should be able to find out who sees your visit.

  • PostHog, hosted in the European Union, counts page views and clicks on our phone, email and booking links. It is set to its cookieless mode: instead of storing an identifier on your device, the server works one out from the day, your network address and your browser type. That value is rebuilt every day, so it cannot be used to follow you from one day to the next. Session recording and surveys are switched off.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics reports how quickly pages loaded and which pages were visited. Cloudflare adds it at their end, so it is not in our website code. It uses no cookies.

If you press Reject on our cookie banner, or turn off visit measurement in your cookie choices, we stop PostHog from collecting anything about you. Cloudflare Web Analytics is added by our hosting rather than by our pages, so we cannot switch it off per visitor; your browser's Do Not Track setting is honoured by PostHog.

Other companies your browser contacts

Some of the fonts, icons and code libraries this site uses are served by other companies. Your browser has to ask them for those files, so they can see your network address and which page you were on. They do not set cookies on our pages. They are:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, security and the font and code libraries served from cdnjs.cloudflare.com
  • Google — the Montserrat web font (fonts.googleapis.com)
  • Fonticons — the Font Awesome icons (use.fontawesome.com)
  • jsDelivr — the Bootstrap code library (cdn.jsdelivr.net)
  • Microsoft — the live chat, but only once you have allowed it

How to change your mind

Use the Cookie choices link in the footer of any page, or the button near the top of this page. Your choice is remembered for 12 months, after which we will ask again.

Turning the live chat off stops it loading the next time a page opens. It cannot remove cookies Microsoft has already set — to do that, clear cookies in your browser settings. Every major browser also lets you block or delete cookies for a whole site; if you block ours, the site still works, but we will have to ask you about cookies again on every page.
